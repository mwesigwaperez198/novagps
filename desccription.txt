### 3. Core Principles

The project is governed by these principles:

1. Authorization over action
   - Every investigation or recovery workflow requires explicit authority, scope, or ownership.

2. Evidence-first operations
   - All findings and actions must be recorded with chain-of-custody and audit evidence.

3. Privacy preservation
   - Only necessary data is collected and retained.

4. Managed-device only
   - Device tracking, lockdown, and recovery are restricted to enrolled, owned, or lawfully authorized devices.

5. Human oversight
   - High-risk actions require review, approval, and clear documentation.

6. Compliance by design
   - The platform enforces legal, operational, and regulatory boundaries before execution.

7. Security and recovery together
   - The system supports both protective functions and lawful investigative workflows.

### 7. Business and Operational Use Cases

#### Use Case A: Stolen managed phone
- Device is enrolled in the system
- Owner reports theft
- Platform validates ownership and case authorization
- Device is placed in lost mode or locked
- Security team reviews last known location and network context
- Device can be quarantined from network access if policy allows
- Report generated for asset recovery and evidence tracking

#### Use Case B: Lost corporate laptop
- Serial number and ownership are registered
- Device is flagged as missing
- Remote lock or wipe is triggered under policy
- Last-known IP, geofence, and asset telemetry are collected
- Device risk is assessed for breach or exfiltration risk

#### Use Case C: Device repair analysis
- Device is handed to team under lawful intake
- Security team checks open ports, firmware integrity, network exposure, and app behavior
- Threat indicators and vulnerabilities are documented
- Repair and hardening recommendations are delivered

#### Use Case D: Lawful investigation
- A case is opened by authorized investigators
- Scope, data sources, and legal basis are entered
- Digital evidence is gathered under strict process
- Timeline and findings are reported for review
